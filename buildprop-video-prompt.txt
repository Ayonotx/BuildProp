Create a 60-second professional corporate product advertisement video for "BuildProp" — a construction and real estate management software platform built for the Ghanaian market.

STYLE: Modern corporate tech ad. Dark navy blue and orange color scheme. Clean, minimal, professional. Think Stripe or Notion product videos. Smooth transitions between scenes. Upbeat corporate electronic background music.

VOICEOVER: Use a professional male voice with a confident, warm, African English accent. Narrate the following script throughout the video:

VOICEOVER SCRIPT:
"Managing construction projects shouldn't be this hard. Scattered spreadsheets, missed deadlines, and invisible cash flow are costing you money every single day.

Introducing BuildProp — the all-in-one platform for construction and real estate management.

Track every project. Control every dollar. Manage every person. From milestones and budgets to invoicing, payroll, and HR — everything connected in one powerful dashboard.

And with built-in AI, you can ask questions in plain English and get instant answers about your projects, finances, and operations — even when you're offline.

Choose from three editions. Standard for core features. Premium with full AI intelligence. Or try the Demo with realistic sample data before you buy.

Built for Ghana — GHS currency, multi-branch support across Accra, Kumasi, and Takoradi, SSNIT compliance, and full offline capability.

BuildProp. Start managing smarter."

SCENE BREAKDOWN (show these visuals during the narration):

Scene 1 (0-7 seconds): Show construction sites, cranes, modern buildings being built. Golden hour lighting. Camera moving through steel beams. Text overlay: "BuildProp" in large white text with orange accent, subtitle "Construction & Real Estate Management Platform"

Scene 2 (7-14 seconds): Show a frustrated office worker at a messy desk with papers everywhere, multiple old monitors, chaotic energy. Dramatic lighting. Text overlay: "Managing Projects Shouldn't Be This Hard"

Scene 3 (14-22 seconds): Show a sleek modern laptop or tablet opening to reveal a clean dashboard interface with charts, KPIs, and project data. Orange and blue UI elements. Text overlay: "One Platform. Everything Connected."

Scene 4 (22-30 seconds): Split into three quick shots: (1) Construction site aerial view with workers reviewing plans on tablet — text "Project Management" (2) Hands counting Ghana Cedis currency at a modern desk — text "Finance & Accounting" (3) Diverse team meeting in modern conference room — text "People & Operations"

Scene 5 (30-40 seconds): Show futuristic AI visualization — glowing neural network, holographic brain, or person chatting with an AI on a laptop screen. Purple and blue tech aesthetic. Text overlay: "AI Intelligence That Never Sleeps" with smaller text "Groq Cloud • Ollama Offline • Smart Insights"

Scene 6 (40-48 seconds): Show three product boxes or cards side by side on dark surface with dramatic spotlighting — silver, gold/orange, and purple. Text overlay: "Choose Your Edition" with labels "Standard | Premium | Demo"

Scene 7 (48-54 seconds): Show beautiful aerial shot of Accra skyline at golden hour, Ghanaian flags, modern buildings. Text overlay: "Built for Ghana" with bullet points "GHS Currency • Multi-Branch • SSNIT Compliance • Works Offline"

Scene 8 (54-60 seconds): Show modern glass building at sunset, camera pulling back. Text overlay: "BuildProp" in large text, "Start Managing Smarter" below, and "www.buildprop.com" at the bottom. Orange glow effect on the text.

ADDITIONAL SETTINGS:
- Aspect ratio: 16:9 (landscape, YouTube style)
- Duration: 60 seconds
- Music: Upbeat corporate tech background music, building energy throughout
- Transitions: Smooth cross-fades between scenes
- Text animations: Clean fade-in, professional typography
- Overall mood: Confident, modern, trustworthy, innovative
