# BuildProp — 60-Second Product Video Script

## Overview
- **Duration:** 60 seconds
- **Tone:** Corporate, confident, forward-thinking
- **Music:** Upbeat electronic/corporate (think: Notion, Linear, Stripe ads)
- **Visual Style:** Dark navy background, orange accents, clean typography, smooth transitions
- **Format:** Each scene fades in, holds for 7-8 seconds, then cross-fades to the next

---

## Scene-by-Scene Breakdown

### Scene 1 — Opening (0:00 – 0:07)
**Visual:** Dark navy background with subtle grid lines. The BuildProp logo fades in at center, followed by the tagline typing out.

**On-Screen Text:**
> **BuildProp**
> Construction & Real Estate Management

**Voiceover (optional):**
> "Introducing BuildProp — the platform built for modern construction and real estate."

**Transition:** Smooth fade to black → fade into Scene 2

## Recording Instructions

1. Open `buildprop-ad.html` in Chrome
2. Press `F11` for fullscreen
3. Press `Space` to pause/play, or `◀` `▶` to navigate manually
4. Record with OBS Studio (free) or Windows Game Bar (Win+G)
5. Export as MP4 at 1080p

## Optional Voiceover

Record the voiceover lines above with any tool (ElevenLabs, your phone, a friend). Layer the audio over the screen recording in CapCut (free) or any video editor.

**Recommended voice:** Male or female, mid-20s to 30s, confident but not aggressive. Think Stripe/Notion product videos.

**Music suggestion:** Search "corporate upbeat" on Artlist.io, Epidemic Sound, or YouTube Audio Library (free).
